## Notes per question

**Q1 (llamadart 0.15.x).** The round-1 lead is confirmed: 0.15.6 is current and has two native backends, llama.cpp GGUF+mmproj and LiteRT-LM `.litertlm`, plus LoRA, JSON-schema structured output and tool calling.
- **Upgrade cost from 0.6.8.** The jump spans several labelled breaking releases: 0.6.1, 0.7.1 (iOS 16.4/macOS 14 minimum), 0.10.0, 0.11.0 (largest API break) and 0.14.0 (ABI). Expect code changes in generation, dispose and tool APIs, plus re-generation of saved sessions.
- **Bundled llama.cpp.** 0.6.8 shipped b8480. After 0.8.21 the changelog pins `llamadart-native` semver tags, so I could not map the current pin (v0.7.0-2) to a llama.cpp b-number.
- **VLM coverage.** Qwen3-VL and MedGemma are never named. Gemma 3 is named only for batch limits. Gemma 4 and Qwen3.5 get the most attention. Qwen3-VL working is plausible through generic mtmd but unverified.
- **GPU backends.** Android Vulkan stays experimental and opt-in, with CPU as the default. There is no OpenCL/Adreno backend. Windows x64 ships CUDA (~700 MiB).
- **LiteRT-LM image input.** Yes, image input goes through the LiteRT-LM backend on native platforms (since 0.8.1) when the bundle declares vision. On that backend LoRA is limited to one adapter at scale 1.0, and Gemma FST constrained decoding is unavailable on iOS.
- **Bus factor.** Low: 78 stars, likely one maintainer, but very high cadence (1,235 commits, issues closed daily).

**Q2 (LiteRT-LM).**
- **Releases.** v0.16.0 Aug 11, v0.17.0 Sep 9, v0.18.0 Oct 6, v0.18.1 Oct 20.
- **Official `.litertlm` builds.** Gemma 4 E2B (2.58 GB, ~1.45 GB memory on iPhone 17 Pro GPU) is confirmed on CPU/GPU for Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Linux. Vision is "loaded on demand", but the card gives no per-platform vision statement or image-token budget. Community VLMs exist: InternVL3.5, Qwen2-VL-2B and Ovis2.5. No Qwen3-VL/Qwen3.5-vision or MedGemma `.litertlm` was found.
- **Constrained decoding.** It reportedly exists (JSON Schema/Regex/Lark, C++ page), but I have only an index snippet. Not verified from official docs.
- **Flutter.** Official status is "Community" only.
- **Round-1 lead check.** The iPhone 17 Pro decode figure of 61.1 tok/s for LiteRT-LM is near the official card's 56.5 tok/s (GPU). That is consistent but a different measurement.

**Q3 (VLM encode/prefill benchmarks).** I found no head-to-head llama.cpp mtmd vs LiteRT-LM vs MLX image-TTFT benchmark.
- **Cactus (vendor numbers).** Gemma-4-E2B 256 px image encode: 0.5 s on iPhone 17 Pro, 1.15 s on iPhone 15 Pro.
- **LiteRT-LM.** The official card gives text prefill only: 2,878 tok/s on iPhone 17 Pro GPU, so a ~1k-token image prompt would prefill in about 0.3 s *if* vision tokens prefill at the text rate (unverified).
- **llama.cpp mtmd.** On CPU-only Android it is slow (~30 s, regressing to ~81 s per the issue snippet). A 2025 arXiv OnePlus 13R study reports 2.4–3 s encode for 3–7B VLMs (older evidence, not fetched).

**Q4 (Apple Foundation Models with images).**
- **Confirmed.** It was announced 2026-06-08 (iOS 27 cycle), and Core AI is positioned as Core ML's successor. The Core AI model tooling needs iOS/macOS 27+, and a WWDC session snippet says Core AI models can be plugged into Foundation Models as custom `LanguageModel`s with custom sampling.
- **Not verified.** Device requirements (Apple Intelligence-capable hardware), context window, Romanian support, whether custom adapters still work, and whether guided generation (@Generable) applies to Core AI custom models.
- **Coverage.** Apple-only, so it does not cover Android or Windows.
- **Side lead.** LiteRT-LM v0.15.0 ships an "Apple Foundation framework adapter" for Gemma 4 multimodal.

**Q5 (other Flutter/cross-platform options).**
- **flutter_edge_ai 2.5.1.** Alive (published this week), MIT, all four targets, multi-engine including LiteRT-LM and llama.cpp. No JSON constraint is documented.
- **Cactus.** Active, but its licence ends free use above USD 2M revenue or funding, and Windows is not listed. This is a significant risk for a commercial app.
- **fllama and llama_cpp_dart.** Not found in this budget.
- **Nexa SDK, RunAnywhere, MLC LLM.** Not checked; budget exhausted.
