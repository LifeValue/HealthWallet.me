## Notes
- C1: The vendor OCRBench numbers themselves were not reproduced anywhere independent. An aggregator (awesomeagents.ai) lists 84.5 with no source, which is not citable. Independent evidence on a different benchmark shows the two models about equal on clean and digital documents and Qwen3.5-2B slightly ahead on real-world photos. This disputes the size of the vendor's lead, not its direction on photos.
- C2: The Format Tax paper (Lee, D'Antoni, Berg-Kirkpatrick, arXiv 2604.03616) adds a nuance. It attributes most of the loss to format-requesting prompt instructions rather than the decoder mask, but agrees that separating reasoning from formatting recovers accuracy. So "reason first, constrain late" is supported by two independent groups, while whether the decoder is the cause is disputed. The −8.7 / −20 point magnitudes were not checked against an independent source.
- C3: Secondary press coverage (MarkTechPost, 2026-01-13, which relays vendor numbers) quotes lab-report extraction macro F1 going from 60% to 78%, and the HF model card as relayed lists EHR Dataset 2 (raw PDF→JSON) at 91.0 vs Gemma 3 4B 84.0. I did not see the "25→64" figure in what I retrieved, so the original claim's numbers may come from a different row or dataset; re-check them against the model card. A Google AI forum thread (discuss.ai.google.dev/t/177891) reports that a Q8_0 quant fails to produce valid JSON on dense pages while BF16 works. That is anecdotal and relevant to on-device use. A JMIR preprint (#95844) evaluates MedGemma 1.5 4B on HealthBench, not on extraction.
- C4: Indirect only. The Raspberry Pi 5 CPU article (raspberrypi.com) and the Jetson Thor CPU run both have LiteRT-LM decoding faster than llama.cpp on CPU, which is consistent in direction with the claim. Google's LiteRT-LM Gemma 4 page lists S26 Ultra CPU ~47 / GPU ~52 tok/s, but that is vendor data with no llama.cpp baseline. llama.cpp lacks Gemma 4 MTP drafter support (per a secondary blog), which could explain part of the gap.
- C5: Absence can only be not contradicted, never proven. Two search phrasings (dataset/benchmark, and scanned lab report→FHIR evaluation) found only tutorials and vendor template extractors (LifeOmic, LlamaParse), with no paired image–FHIR dataset.
- C6: The independent source confirms obsolescence through the SDK availability annotations, not through Apple's wording about the "last release". A 3nsofts guide (2026-07-24, written against the prerelease SDK) still described custom adapters as an iOS 27 capability gated by entitlement; that predates the shipped SDK and is superseded by the 2026-10-05 SDK check. No Apple-confirmed successor for model customisation was found.
