## Notes per question

**Q1. Qwen3.5-2B/4B on-device readiness**
- Mainline llama.cpp has supported Qwen3.5 (dense and MoE, with vision via mtmd/mmproj) since 2026-02-10 (#19468), and the GATED_DELTA_NET ggml op since 2026-02-12 (#19504). The small dense models use the same `qwen35` path, but I found no explicit statement that 0.8B/2B/4B vision was tested in the PR. A third-party commit note says the mmproj must match the checkpoint size.
- GGUF: Unsloth is the reputable source (row 4/5). The Qwen org card links only to community quantizations, not an official GGUF (row 7). I did not check ggml-org or bartowski repos.
- Size versus today's stack: Qwen3.5-2B Q4_K_M + F16 mmproj ≈ 1.95 GB, against about 1.5 GB for Qwen3-VL-2B today, so roughly 0.45 GB more. Most of the increase is the larger mmproj (668 MB). Qwen3.5-4B ≈ 3.4 GB.
- Risks: Vulkan problems are reported (device-lost crash, slower than CPU) and their current status is unverified. This matters for Android and Windows GPU paths. The Metal status of the Gated DeltaNet op was not found. Ollama could not load Qwen3.5 with mmproj as of about March 2026 (Unsloth), but that does not affect a direct llama.cpp integration. I found no phone speed report for Qwen3.5. The recurrent DeltaNet state should, in theory, shrink the KV cache, but this is an unverified belief.
- Qwen3.5-4B doc benchmarks (vendor-reported): OCRBench 85.0, OmniDocBench1.5 86.2, CC-OCR 76.7. DocVQA is not reported. The 2B card was not fetched this round; round-1 figures stand unverified by me.

**Q2. Gemma 4 E2B/E4B**
- E2B GGUF Q4_K_M is 3.11 GB before the mmproj, larger than Qwen3.5-4B Q4_K_M (2.74 GB) because of the per-layer embeddings (5.1B total). E2B therefore lands in the ≥6 GB tier on file size in GGUF form, and E4B (8B total) is likely too large for either tier in GGUF. E4B's size was not fetched: unverified.
- LiteRT-LM is the more phone-friendly path. The E2B .litertlm is 2.58 GB, and measured memory on flagship phones was 0.6–1.7 GB at a 2K context, presumably because the embeddings are memory-mapped (my inference, not stated). These are text-only numbers; vision loads on demand, and image-encode cost and RAM were not reported. Adopting it means a second runtime alongside llama.cpp.
- llama.cpp Gemma 4 vision: implied supported by the ggml-org OCR blog (2026-04-10), and Google's own docs page shows `llama-server -hf ggml-org/gemma-4-E2B-it-GGUF` (search snippet, not fetched).
- Doc benchmarks: only OmniDocBench 1.5 edit distance (E2B 0.290, E4B 0.181; lower is better) and MedXPertQA MM (23.5 / 28.7) are vendor-reported. No DocVQA, OCRBench or ChartQA. The edit-distance metric cannot be compared directly with Qwen's OmniDocBench overall score.

**Q3. Screen of other candidates**
- MiniCPM-V 4.6 (May 2026, about 1B, Apache-2.0, llama.cpp GGUF, phone demos) is a new viable small candidate, but its document numbers are unverified. Earlier MiniCPM-V 4.x/4.5 licences were not checked.
- DeepSeek-OCR is in mainline llama.cpp (Mar 2026). DeepSeek-OCR-2 is not confirmed, and its Vulkan ops are missing. Size and licence were not checked this round.
- dots.ocr, GLM-OCR, LightOnOCR, HunyuanOCR, Qianfan-OCR and PaddleOCR-VL are listed as llama.cpp-supported OCR models (row 12). Sizes and licences were not checked. They are pure OCR/markdown models, which would need a second LLM pass for FHIR coding.
- Granite-Docling-258M: only community GGUFs, quality unverified.
- Not screened (budget ran out): InternVL3.5 1B/2B/4B or newer, SmolVLM2/SmolDocling, olmOCR (7B-based per prior belief, which would likely fail the ≤4B gate: unverified), Moondream 3 (MoE about 9B total per prior belief, which would likely fail the gate: unverified), Granite Vision small.

**Q4. Independent head-to-head**
- None found that covers Qwen3.5-2B/4B vs Qwen3-VL-2B vs Gemma 4 E2B/E4B vs MedGemma 1.5 on OCR, documents or medical documents. Every document number in this digest is vendor-reported. The project will need its own evaluation set (EN/ES/DE/RO medical documents).
