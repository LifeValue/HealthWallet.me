## Notes per question

**Q1. llama.cpp issue status.** As of 2026-10-22:
- Closed as completed: #21228 (fix commit not identified), #20345 (and follow-up #22537), #25746.
- Closed as not_planned: #29690, with the 128-level nesting guard PR #30242 still open.
- Open: #29462 (PR #29497 open) and #29006 (PR #29066 open).
- New and relevant: #30321, where an optional field before a required one gets silently dropped.

I could not identify any build that fixes CVE-2026-52130; treat it as unfixed upstream until PR #30242 merges.

For the app this matters less than it looks. llamadart does **not** call llama.cpp's converter: it ships its own Dart port (F14) with its own depth and repetition guards (F15). The upstream server-side crash and OOM bugs therefore do not apply directly. The design flaws (required-first ordering, repetition thresholds) are still worth testing in the port.

Grammar + mtmd: an issue search for "grammar mtmd" returned nothing relevant (only version bumps and build issues). I found no evidence either way.

**Q2. The two arXiv papers.**
- Constraint tax: real and large at ≤3B. Validity goes to 100% while accuracy drops 8.7 pts in aggregate and up to 20 pts per model, with a 43.5-pt drop on the tool-call task.
- Mitigations: "reason free, constrain late", or a rationale field before the answer (36.5% against 26.8%).
- DCCD (draft without constraints, then constrain with the draft in context): up to +24 pts at 1B, and +24.6 pts for 1.5B on GSM8K. Both papers use reasoning and maths tasks, not document extraction, so transfer to OCR→FHIR is a hypothesis.
- Neither paper tests field ordering, short keys or enums. The closest evidence is CFG4FHIR (relaxed key ordering helped validity; enums are enforced but can be "valid and wrong") and llama.cpp #30321 (ordering interacts with the grammar).

**Q3. CFG4FHIR.**
- Method: FHIR (R5) → Pydantic JSON Schema (`fhir.resources` 7.1.0) → jq-filtered schema → custom CFG with relaxed key order → EBNF/GBNF/xgrammar-GBNF.
- Results: 76.5% / 41.2% / 17.6% validity (CFG / JSON-schema / unguided) on 17 cases.
- Model and scale: the reported cases ran on Llama 3.3 70B, so there is **no small-model evidence**.
- Licence: the paper is CC BY-NC 4.0; the repo has no licence, so ask the author before reusing the grammar.
- Version gap: their grammar is R5, and the app targets R4.

**Q4. llamadart.** It accepts raw GBNF and `responseFormat` JSON schema, converted to GBNF by its own Dart port of llama.cpp's converter. I found no llguidance support. LiteRT-LM has no grammar constraints (#174 open) and fails strictly instead of degrading silently.

**Q5. FHIR post-validation.** `fhir_r4` + `fhir_r4_validation` 0.14.0 (fresh, 2026-10-16) give on-device Dart validation of structure, cardinality, bindings and invariants. Note that the models are **R4B**, not R4. I did not verify whether binding validation works offline or needs a terminology server. I did not research the HAPI FHIR validator CLI because the budget ran out; it is in any case a JVM tool and not usable on-device. Evidence on XGrammar on mobile is thin (F19).
